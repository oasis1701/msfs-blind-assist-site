**Flying Microsoft Flight Simulator with a screen reader.** Covers the released version of MSFS Blind Assist as of 18 October 2026.

The guide is one page per chapter. On every chapter page the chapter title is the only heading at level 1, sections are level 2 and subsections level 3, so with a screen reader you can press H for the next heading, or 2 and 3 to move by level. Each chapter opens with an "In this chapter" list of links to its sections, and ends with links to the previous and next chapters. The contents below list every part and chapter.

## Part 7: Aircraft Guides {#part-7-aircraft-guides}

Everything you have read so far works in every aircraft. This part covers what changes in each one: how BA lays out its cockpit, the special windows it adds, its own callouts, and the quirks worth knowing before your first flight. The full key list for each aircraft is in the [Hotkey Reference]({{ '/guide/hotkey-reference/' | relative_url }}) and in File, Hotkey List; the sections below pick out the highlights.

These guides do not teach you to fly the aircraft itself. For engine starts, flows and speeds, use the maker's own documentation; the standard procedures it describes are exactly what you carry out through BA's panels. [General Flying Concepts]({{ '/guide/general-flying-concepts/' | relative_url }}) gives a gentle overview of airliner flying.
